- Wording such as “corrected EX rating”, “sight test document”, and “in accordance documentation” is retained as printed; no technical corrections were invented.
- No unresolved section or grey-cell mapping remains after the two-page visual check. Protection-group labels follow the PDF's merged cells, not guesses from question text. Actual standard currency and technical suitability still require the independent reviewer.
